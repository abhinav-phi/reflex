## Try the live system first

The full product runs publicly with seeded demo data ([SIMULATED], test mode):

- **UI:** https://reflex-recover.vercel.app
- **Login (password `reflex-demo`):** `admin@reflex.dev` · `approver@reflex.dev` ·

- **Operator runbook:** [MANUAL_STEPS.md](../MANUAL_STEPS.md) — setup, demo playbook,
  failure injections, troubleshooting

## Get support

1. Open a new issue and describe what you attempted and what you expected — include the
   page/endpoint, the exact error text, and whether it was on the live deployment or a
   local run.
2. For evaluation-protocol questions, read [eval/PROTOCOL.md](../eval/PROTOCOL.md) and
   [docs/limitations.md](../docs/limitations.md) first — most metric questions are
   answered there (including honestly-reported gate misses).
3. Security-sensitive findings must **not** go through public issues — see
   [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
